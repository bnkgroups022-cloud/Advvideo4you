|---|---|---|---|

## Music

The background tracks are generated by `advvideo/music.py` at run time. They are original, contain no third-party recording, and are released under CC0.

## What is deliberately not used

`facebook/nllb-200` (CC BY-NC) and other non-commercial models were avoided so that the default pipeline has no non-commercial dependency other than the Hindi voice.
